About us

Who we are

A coral restoration program built on science, powered by community.

Our mission

Restore. Involve. Research.

Reef Response’s mission is to stimulate coral reef ecosystem health through coral restoration, promote community involvement through citizen science, and ensure best practices through scientific research.

Our goals

  1. Increase abundance and diversity

    Grow the number and variety of reef-building corals across the U.S. Virgin Islands.

  2. Involve the community

    Expand community participation in coral reef conservation and research.

  3. Advance the science

    Collect scientific data that improves the efficacy of coral restoration practices.

Citizen science

Citizen scientists make this possible

Reef Response is part of the University of the Virgin Islands and has a team of highly trained scientific divers. However, our team is always in need of assistance with coral nursery maintenance and monitoring. Corals are delicate animals and require great care to grow and thrive.

Our program depends on public support, which is why citizen scientists are vital to our mission. Citizen scientists are crucial team members in our program and ultimately make it possible to monitor and maintain large numbers of corals.

Our citizen scientists directly contribute to the health and growth of the USVI coral reef communities that provide important ecological, economic, and social services for the islands. The data they collect serves as an integral tool in our restoration success.

Report signs of bleaching

Reef Response, along with our colleagues and collaborators, consistently monitors the health of coral reefs around St. Thomas. But we can’t do this all by ourselves.

If you notice anything unusual on your snorkel or dive, we would appreciate if you take a moment to fill out a coral health report survey. This is crucial to inform us of where disease mitigation is needed. In addition, this information will be referenced in scoping future restoration sites.
