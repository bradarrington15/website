Outreach

Sharing coral reef science with the next generation of Virgin Islanders — in classrooms, at public events, and out on the reef.

Launched spring 2026

Corals in the Classroom

We partner with local high school biology teachers to bring coral reef ecology straight into USVI classrooms.

Corals in the Classroom is a four-lesson curriculum designed for high school students. The lessons introduce students to coral ecosystems, coral restoration, and scientific jargon — and to how they can become involved, both as high school students and in their future careers.

Launched in spring 2026, the curriculum is currently taught at Charlotte Amalie High School, and we hope to expand it to more local schools in the future.

In the classroom

What the lessons cover

Four lessons, designed for high school students, that take them from how a reef works to how they could one day work on one.

Lesson 1

Coral Basics and ID

What corals are, how they live and grow, and how to tell the different species apart.

Lesson 2

Ecosystem Services of Coral Reefs

The benefits reefs provide — from fisheries and tourism to protecting coastlines and storing carbon.

Lesson 3

Understanding Coral Reef Health

How to recognize healthy and stressed coral, and the bleaching, disease, and other threats reefs face.

Lesson 4

Conservation and Restoration of Coral Reefs

How reefs are protected and restored — including Reef Response’s own nursery work — and how students can get involved.
