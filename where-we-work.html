Where we work

Nurseries, outplanting sites, and monitoring stations across St. Thomas, St. John, and the surrounding cays.

Reef Response works at 24 locations around the U.S. Virgin Islands — from the land-based nursery at UVI’s Center for Marine and Environmental Studies to in-water nurseries, outplanting sites, and long-term monitoring reefs across two islands.
