The program

What Reef Responders do

Our citizen scientists are crucial team members — they make it possible to monitor and maintain large numbers of corals.

Needs content — Reef Responder program description

Describe the program in detail: who can join (certification level, age, swim/dive ability), what a typical dive day looks like, time commitment, training provided, and any cost or equipment requirements.

  1. Nursery maintenance

    Clean nursery structures to keep algae, sponges, tunicates, and barnacles from overgrowing the corals.

  2. Nursery monitoring

    Record growth, mortality, and health indicators for corals in our in-water nurseries.

  3. Outplant monitoring

    Track how corals we’ve returned to the reef are faring over time.
